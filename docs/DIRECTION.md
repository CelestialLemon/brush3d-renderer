# Art direction

Decided 2026-10-29 with the developer, from *Dear Alice* (reference/), Sable, Breath of the Wild, Terra Nil and Tiny Glade.

## The rule: painted world, inked things

- **Inked**: anything the player placed, and anything that moves. Buildings, machines, vehicles, goods, a field's bed and frame.
- **Painted**: anything that grows, and the ground. Terrain and earthworks, grass, crops (even in an inked field), trees and
  saplings (even planted ones), water.

## The ink

- Lines in a darker, richer shade of the surface's own colour, never black.
- Silhouettes and major creases only; lines thin out as the camera zooms out. Clean and calm, not a comic.
- Cel shading in 2-3 crisp bands, the shade leaning to the hour's hue.

## The paint

- Colour, not flatness: one meadow runs yellow-green to emerald to teal; shadows are saturated teal or violet, never grey.
- Brush marks keyed to the world, never the screen: the paint must not slide as the camera pans, zooms or turns.
- Foliage as clumps with crown-shaped normals, so the bands fall in soft blobs; gold rim light on the sun side.
- Distance fades into blue haze. Weather and the hour should change the whole picture (fog, dust, frost, golden hour).

## The vibe

Clean and serene. A full farm must stay calm: ink is kept for the few things that matter, the land carries the colour.
